Selected Research & Engineering Projects

Cavitation & failure of flexible-display adhesives

Designed peel and confined-layer tests and image-processing methods to track cavities and fingers. Combined viscoelastic parameter identification with finite element analysis to connect local damage evolution to mechanical response.

Thermal interface materials for chip packaging

Characterized compression, cyclic loading and rheology. Identified viscoelastic and softening behavior; compared simulations with experiments to assess alignment, size effects and warpage-related softening and recovery.

Curing & dynamic response of camera damping adhesives

Supported UV-curing optimization and developed a high-speed drop-weight impact test platform. Used frequency sweeps, time–temperature superposition and relaxation tests for constitutive modeling and coupon/module validation.

Switchable adhesion of epidermal electrodes

Studied skin–electrode co-deformation and direction-dependent adhesion. Co-first author of the related Advanced Materials publication (2026).
